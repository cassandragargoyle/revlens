# 2 Risks

Three risks are live. The rest were carried forward from the feasibility study, were
re-read in April, and are closed.

**Counts disagree at cutover.** Two ledgers are written to for a week and the old one
stays on until a full count agrees with the new one. If the counts do not agree, the wave
stops where it is; nothing is switched off on a promise.

**The regional teams keep their own spreadsheets.** This is the one risk no interface
closes. Each site keeps its spreadsheet until the ledger answers the question that
spreadsheet was built to answer, and the ledger's backlog is ordered by that.

**The vendor delays the interface.** The contract ties the second payment to the
interface, not to the date, and wave one needs no interface at all.
