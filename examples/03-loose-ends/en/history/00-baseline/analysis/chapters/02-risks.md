# 2 Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact |
| ---- | ---------- | ------ |
| Stock counts disagree at cutover | medium | high |
| The regional teams keep their own spreadsheets | high | medium |
| The ledger vendor delays the interface | low | high |
